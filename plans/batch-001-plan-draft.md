## 2. Reality check (as promised in D-79)
### Marketing: realistic, with changes
- **Volume:** 37 days is about 37 items: 16 to 21 videos and 16 to 21 posts. A marketing skill check says 3 to 5 posts a week is the usual LinkedIn rhythm. 7 a week is above it, so quality and your review time are the risk, not reach.
- **Reach goals are easy:** 10 people per post and 100 to 200 people for the month are low if the page keeps posting. A good goal to add later is followers gained and clicks to the trial page.
- **Measure:** the weekly LinkedIn analytics export. If it gives only impressions and not unique people, I report impressions and tell you.
- **Add tracking:** every link to tickflowai.com uses a tracking tag (UTM), so trials that came from LinkedIn can be counted. Without it we cannot say "trials from marketing".
- **What to make:** our competitor scan found that people and problem posts get far more engagement than plain product videos. So videos start with one real IT pain, are short, have English subtitles and a hook in the first 3 seconds. Mix: problem videos, product-in-action (WhatsApp, Teams, AI), people and behind-the-scenes from the company page, and real questions.
- **Cost:** videos are the expensive part. Plan: build ONE reusable Remotion template in week 1, then each video is a new script and text. Rendering uses no plan usage.
- **Rule:** if quality or the $20 usage suffers, we cut to 5 or 6 items a week, and you decide.

### Sales: contacts and demos realistic, replies are a stretch
- **Real benchmark data (vendor datasets, ranges only):** average cold email reply rate is about 3.4%, good is 5.5% and above, excellent is 10% and above. Meetings are about 0.5% to 1% of contacts, good is 1% to 2%.
- So 100 contacts: about 3 to 6 replies is normal, 10 to 20 would be excellent or above. **1 to 5 demos is realistic at the low end (1 to 2), 5 is a stretch.** Your goal stays as the target. Replies from LinkedIn messages that founders send by hand may help.
- **Ramp (slow start, D-60):** week 1 prepare, no sending. Then about 10, 20, 25, 30 and 15 contacts a week. That reaches about 100 by 30 Dec.
- **Lead research:** about 130 to 150 leads checked, so that 100 are Go.
- **Deliverability rules from the skill:** verified emails only (bounce under 2%), no open tracking, opt-out line and postal address in every email, stop on any reply. The skill also advises a separate sending domain. You decided on connect@ with a slow start (D-59), so I keep that and watch bounces and complaints closely. The stop line stops sending if they rise.
- **Your time:** about 100 messages to review and send in 4 weeks is about 25 a week.

### Automation: too big as written, needs a smaller "done"
- Today (from your plan): the RBAC catalog is approved. The other modules have no catalog yet. Automation (Stage 3) has not started. About 12 modules exist.
- "Finish functionality and end-to-end UI for everything" in 5 weeks, with about 20% of the usage, is not realistic. I would not promise it.
- **Proposal:** define "done" per module: catalog approved by you, functional tests automated, end-to-end UI flows automated, each test passes 3 runs in a row. Then pick the module order. Target for 30 Dec: the first 4 to 5 modules done. I will give a better number after week 1, when I can measure real speed.
- Performance and security runs are not in these goals, so they are not in this batch.
