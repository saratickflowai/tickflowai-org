## 5. Rules for this company
- A founder sends every email from connect@ and every LinkedIn message by hand. No agent sends.
- Verified emails only, no open tracking, opt-out line and postal address in every email, stop on any reply.
- Lead personal data lives only in HubSpot. Never in git, chat, documents or logs.
- Only public prices (Core and Pro). Never a discount. Enterprise price and contract terms only from both founders.
- Lead history: a minimal record of every lead not continued, with the reason, is kept forever (D-88).
