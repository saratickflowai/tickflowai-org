## 4. Rules for this company
- One idea, one tone per item. The same idea is never used twice, or in two tones, in the same week (D-102).
- No question or poll posts until the page has an audience (D-101). Posts end with a clear next step.
- Only Live features from the Product Fact Sheet. Only public prices. No competitor names. No invented numbers.
- The Remotion template is the frame only. Story, words, screens and voice are new in every video.
- Links go in the first comment with a tracking tag.
- If quality or usage suffers, the Director proposes 5 or 6 items a week. Sara decides.
