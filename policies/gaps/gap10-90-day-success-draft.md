# Gap G10: What result after 90 days means "keep going" - DRAFT v0.1

**Date:** 2026-11-24. **Status:** Draft for discussion. Nothing is decided.

## The gap
The plan has no test for the organization itself. Without one, we cannot decide when to pay for more capacity (a bigger plan), change a company, or stop.

## Proposal

### Check points
Day 30, day 60 and day 90 after the first batch starts. Each check point is a short report to both founders, built from records only (Gap 9 rules).

### What we check (outcomes, not activity)
| Area | Question | Example of a clear result |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Is the company page active with approved content, and do people react? | Steady posts and finished videos, real reactions, and founders approve most drafts without changes. |
| Leads and Sales | Do we have verified leads, and do they answer? | Leads checked and sorted in HubSpot, outreach sent after approval, replies, demos booked, trials started. |
| Quality Engineering | Does automation protect each deploy? | Regression suite runs on every deploy, valid bugs only, zero duplicates, one performance run and one security run done. |
| Founders' time | Does the system save you time? | Time you spend reviewing and approving is less than the time the work would take by hand. |
| Cost | Do we stay inside the budget? | Within the $20 plan, with planned vs actual usage shown in every batch report. |

### Numbers
I do not invent targets. We have no baseline today. Suggestion: **no hard targets for the first 30 days.** Day 30 gives real numbers (posts, leads, replies, approval rate, usage). Then Sara sets the targets for day 60 and day 90 with those numbers in front of her.

### Decision at day 90, for each company
- **Continue:** results are on target, keep the same size.
- **Adjust:** some results are good, some are not; change the plan or the agents.
- **Pause:** results are weak or cost more than they give; stop that company and keep the files.
- **Grow:** results are good and the usage limit is the problem; consider a bigger plan (D-20 and the Max plan idea).
Both founders decide (money and direction).
