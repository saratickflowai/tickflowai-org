# Gap G3: Nobody is responsible after the sale - DRAFT v0.1

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Status:** Draft for discussion. Nothing here is decided yet.

## The gap
The original document ends at the proposal. Nobody owns what happens after a company starts a trial or buys. For a ticketing product, support is part of the product. The first customers will judge us on how we help them.

## What this function would cover (my suggestion)
1. **Trial follow-up:** a check-in during the 14-day trial (for example near the start, in the middle, and before the end), so trials turn into paying customers.
2. **Onboarding:** welcome, setup help, first-week check.
3. **Support:** answer customer questions. Bugs go to Abdelrahman through the normal bug process.
4. **Renewals and upgrades:** renewal dates, seat limits, Core to Pro upgrades, Enterprise topics (founders only).
5. **Feedback loop:** feature requests to the developer, quotes and case studies to Marketing (always with approval).
6. **Early warning:** customers who stop using the product.

## How it fits our rules
- It would be one agent, started by a trigger (a new trial, a new paying customer, a support message, a renewal date). It does nothing otherwise.
- Anything sent to a customer follows the approval rules, until the founders decide otherwise.
- Every risk is shown to both founders (D-36).

## Updates from Sara (2026-11-22)
- The product already sends trial emails on a schedule. The agent does not send these.
- Setup help is limited.
- There is a Support inbox for support messages, and a Connect inbox for reaching leads and sending proposals.
- Renewals: later (handled from the admin portal by the founders).
- The function stays dormant until the first paying client arrives.
- Shared inboxes (Support and Content) are visible to both founders. The agent drafts only. Bugs go to the board. Non-standard questions wait for a founder's real answer.
