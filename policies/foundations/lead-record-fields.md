# Lead Record: All Possible Fields (for Sara to choose)

**Where it lives:** HubSpot (the CRM). Personal data stays only in HubSpot, never in git or in reports.
**Rules for every field:** it must have a source and a collection date. If no source gives it, it stays empty. Agents never invent data.

Status: Sara approved this list on 2026-11-22. Any field can be removed or added later.

## A. Where the lead came from
1. Connector or tool: Apollo, Apify, Vibe Prospecting, ScrapeGraphAI
2. Source page link (URL)
3. Date collected
4. Collected by (agent name)
5. Batch ID
6. Confidence per field (high / medium / low)

## B. Company
7. Company name (and legal name)
8. Main website
9. Country
10. City
11. Full address of head office
12. Other branches or sites
13. Industry and sub-industry
14. Segment (regulated sector, technology, other)
15. Company size (number of employees) and size band
16. Estimated revenue
17. Year founded
18. Parent group or ownership
19. LinkedIn company page
20. Company phone
21. General company email
22. Other social pages

## C. IT setup
23. Has an IT team? (yes / no / unknown)
24. IT team size
25. Name of the head of IT
26. Current ticketing tool (if any)
27. Other tools they use (Microsoft 365, Teams, Google Workspace, WhatsApp, ...)
28. How they handle IT requests today (email, WhatsApp, Excel, tool)
29. Estimated agent seats
30. Plan fit (Core, Pro or Enterprise)
31. Open IT job posts

## D. Problems and timing
32. Main problem they face
33. Evidence for it, with source link
34. Trigger events (hiring, expansion, complaints, news)
35. Competitor tool they use, and contract renewal date if known

## E. Decision maker
36. Full name
37. Job title
38. Role in the decision (decision maker, influencer, user, gatekeeper)
39. Seniority and department
40. LinkedIn profile
41. Work email
42. Mobile phone
43. Direct or office phone
44. Other ways to reach them (WhatsApp, X, ...)
45. Preferred channel
46. Language and time zone
47. How we are connected (mutual contacts, shared groups)
48. Earlier contact with us

## F. Other people at the company
49. Second contact (name, title, how to reach)
50. IT manager or service desk lead (name, title, how to reach)

## G. Score and decision
51. ICP fit score
52. Reasons for the score
53. Priority tier
54. Group: Go, Skip, or Founders decide
55. Reason when the group is "Founders decide"

## H. Sales tracking (what each field means)
| # | Field | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 56 | Assigned founder | The one human (Sara or the co-founder) who is responsible for this lead. They approve the messages for it and take the demo. Agents prepare the work, but this person owns the lead. |
| 57 | Outreach status | Where we are in contacting them: not contacted, message approved, message sent, replied, no reply, not interested. |
| 58 | Last contact date | The last date anyone (founder or agent) was in touch with them. |
| 59 | Next follow-up date | The date of the next planned action, so no lead is forgotten. |
| 60 | Replies and notes | What they answered, and notes from the agents or founders (objections, questions, interest). |
| 61 | Demo status | Not offered, offered, booked, done, or no show. |

## I. Data quality
62. Email verified? (yes / no) and date
63. Phone verified? and date
64. Duplicate check result
65. Last verified date
66. Do not contact flag
