## Part 2. Tone options (same message, three voices)

The message in every sample: *employees can create and continue tickets over WhatsApp, and voice notes become text (Pro and Enterprise plans).*

### Tone 1: Expert and practical
Short sentences, concrete, no hype, shows how it works.
- **LinkedIn post:** "Your IT team gets requests on WhatsApp. Some arrive as voice notes. Many never become a ticket. With TickFlowAI, an employee sends a WhatsApp message, and it becomes a ticket. A voice note is turned into text. The agent continues the conversation in the same ticket. Question: how do requests reach your IT team today?"
- **Cold email opening:** "Hello [Name], many IT teams in [country] get requests by WhatsApp and email, and track them in Excel. TickFlowAI puts them in one ticket list, with AI suggestions for each reply. Would a short demo be useful?"
- **Video hook (first 3 seconds, on screen):** "A voice note on WhatsApp. A ticket in two seconds." *(Do not use a time claim unless we measure it. Replace with: "A voice note on WhatsApp becomes a ticket.")*
- **Use:** plain verbs (create, track, assign, answer), real screens, one idea per post.
- **Avoid:** hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), vague AI talk ("AI agents"), unmeasured numbers.

### Tone 2: Friendly
Warm, talks to "you", light and human.
- **LinkedIn post:** "Be honest: how many of your IT requests start as a WhatsApp message or a voice note? We've all been there. With TickFlowAI, that message becomes a ticket, and the voice note turns into text. Your team keeps the whole chat in one place. How does your team handle it today? Tell us below."
- **Cold email opening:** "Hi [Name], quick hello from the TickFlowAI team. Lots of IT teams tell us their requests live in WhatsApp. We built a simple way to turn those messages into tickets. Happy to show you in 15 minutes if you're curious."
- **Video hook:** "Your IT inbox is a WhatsApp chat? Same."
- **Use:** "you", short questions, small stories from IT teams.
- **Avoid:** slang, jokes about customers, too many emojis, anything that sounds like a promise we cannot prove.

### Tone 3: Formal
Polished and careful, suits banks, insurers and large organizations.
- **LinkedIn post:** "IT service requests increasingly arrive through messaging channels. TickFlowAI enables employees to raise and continue tickets through WhatsApp, with voice notes converted to text, so that every request is recorded, assigned and traceable. We welcome your views on how your organization manages these requests."
- **Cold email opening:** "Dear [Title] [Name], organizations in [sector] often receive IT requests through several channels. TickFlowAI consolidates them in a single ticketing system with audit logging and SLA tracking. We would be glad to arrange a short demonstration at your convenience."
- **Video hook:** "One system for every IT request."
- **Use:** full sentences, "organization", "enables", clear structure.
- **Avoid:** contractions, slang, casual questions.

## Part 3. Rules for every tone (already agreed)
- English first. Videos with English subtitles.
- Company page only, never Sara's personal profile.
- No competitor names, no price comparison, no discounts, only public prices.
- Only Live features; nothing about Planned features.
- No invented numbers or results.
- Posts start with a real IT pain, end with a real question.
