# Founder Decision Rights (Gap G2) - DRAFT v0.2

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Status:** Draft built from Sara's answers. Not final.
**Founders:** Sara Magdy (QA lead, also responsible for Marketing) and Abdelrahman Sameh (developer, co-founder). Confirmed by Sara on 2026-11-22: the developer and the co-founder are the same person.
**Where it applies:** Marketing and Sales (shared). Quality Engineering is Sara's area.

## 1. Who approves what

| Type of item | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Routine items that follow an approved template (regular posts, follow-up messages, replies) | Either founder |
| New campaign | Either founder or both |
| New message or content template | Either founder or both |
| **Price, discount, contract, Enterprise terms** | **Both founders** |
| **Anything that costs money** (for example a paid tool) | **Both founders** |
| **Anything with legal risk** | **Both founders.** It is treated as Critical. |
| Demos and live talks | Both usually lead. See section 2. |
| Critical security findings | Sara alone |
| A routine item that is stuck | Goes back to both founders |

## 2. The "record" and "skip" buttons
They appear on every item that needs both founders, and on demos. **Both founders can see them and both can use them.**
- **Record:** a founder enters an opinion or approval. This can be their own, or the other founder's (for example, given by phone).
- **Skip:** a founder skips the other founder's opinion on this item.

Rules for both buttons:
1. A written **reason** is always required.
2. Every record or skip is saved with who did it, when, and why.
3. Both founders can see all records and skips, and they appear in the report.
4. Skipping is allowed on money items too. It is saved and visible, so it is the founders' call.

## 3. If the founders disagree
The system shows who approved and who did not. The founders decide together, then the decision is entered in the system.

## 4. If a founder is away
The record and skip buttons cover it.

## 5. When an approval stops being valid
- When the content is edited after approval, it needs approval again.
- Default: **7 days** for everything. If something is planned further ahead, the founders can set a different time for it. The default can be changed at any time.

## 6. Reminders (dynamic, by importance)
| Importance | Default reminder |
|---|---|
| Routine item | After about a week |
| Important item (a campaign that must start, a contract to finish, a discount to send, an Enterprise topic to discuss before a demo) | After 1 working day. Never more than 2 days. |

Starting defaults. The founders adjust them together. Each founder can have different reminders, depending on what each one sees and how important it is.

## 7. Other rules that depend on this
- Abdelrahman writes the bug fixes. Sara (QA) verifies that a fix is correct. A developer is never the only approver of his own fix.

## 8. Risk visibility (Sara, 2026-11-22)
Every risk, of any level, must be known by **both founders**. Agents send each risk to both founders, and it appears in both founders' reports. This includes critical security findings: Sara decides them alone, but Abdelrahman is always told.
