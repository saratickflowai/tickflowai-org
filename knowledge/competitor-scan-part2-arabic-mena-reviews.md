## What this means for us (internal ideas, not claims)
1. **Arabic is not a safe "only us" point.** ManageEngine and ServiceNow both list Arabic, and Zoho's parent says it supports Arabic and right-to-left. Our edge must be depth and simplicity (Arabic across screens, emails, notifications, reports, AI drafts and Arabic-aware search), not "we have Arabic". Positioning 4.2 must not claim that others lack Arabic (rule 4.4b stays).
2. **WhatsApp:** only Zoho showed it. For Freshservice, ManageEngine and ServiceNow the pages read did not say. Our WhatsApp tickets with voice notes and sentiment is a strong point, but "competitors lack WhatsApp" is still not allowed.
3. **Regional presence:** the Zoho and ManageEngine family is investing in the region (Amman office Jul 2026, Saudi data centres). Expect strong local sales presence from them in the Gulf.
4. **Reviews:** Freshservice has the best rating of the three that were checked. Our reviews are zero today, so early customer stories matter.
5. **Price and ratings together:** the cheapest entry (ManageEngine) has the lowest G2 rating of the three.
