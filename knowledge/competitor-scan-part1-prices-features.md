## Per product notes
**Freshservice.** Starter $19, Growth $49, Pro $99, Enterprise custom, all per agent per month billed annually. Monthly prices not shown. Starter has incident, knowledge and task management. Growth adds service catalog and SLA management. Pro adds problem, change and release management. Enterprise adds audit logs and sandbox. Same entry price as our Core ($19), but their SLA and service catalog start at $49, while our Core has SLA on tickets and our Pro ($45) has the catalog.

**ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus.** Prices are "from" figures, per technician, and Professional and Enterprise are also per IT asset. The page says prices are current as of September 2026. Cloud examples: Standard from $1,545 per year for 10 technicians; Professional from $645 per year for 2 technicians and 250 assets. Add-ons cost extra (service catalog, remote access). Support 24x5 included. Cheapest entry of the four, but the total depends on assets and add-ons.

**Zoho Desk.** The page showed Indian rupees; USD plan prices were not in the text. Free edition (3 users). Express, Standard, Professional, Enterprise. WhatsApp and other messaging from Standard. Many extras (telephony, AI) sit on higher plans. Light agents cost extra.

**ServiceNow ITSM.** Quote only: Foundation, Advanced, Prime. No prices or trial on the page. Heavy AI focus. This is the enterprise end of the market.

## What this means for us (internal ideas, not claims)
1. Our Core price equals Freshservice Starter. Our Pro is cheaper than Freshservice Growth and Pro, but Freshservice Growth has SLA and catalog, and we include more per tier. Compare features line by line before saying anything, and never publicly.
2. ManageEngine looks cheaper at the start, but add-ons and per-asset pricing can raise the total.
3. No competitor page showed Arabic support. This needs Part 2 before we use it.
4. Teams support exists at Freshservice. The claim "competitors lack Teams" stays forbidden (Positioning 4.4b).
