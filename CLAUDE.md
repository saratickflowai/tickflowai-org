# tickflowai-org (private)

Documents and agent definitions for the TickFlowAI AI-agent organization (Marketing, Sales, Quality Engineering). All files are DRAFT until Sara marks them Final v1.

## Rules for every session
- Documents in simple English. Chat with Sara in simple English, not mixed with Arabic.
- Nothing goes out (post, email, message, publish) without founder approval. Agents write drafts.
- Never store passwords, keys, tokens, lead or customer personal data in this repo. Lead data lives only in HubSpot.
- Only public prices. No discounts. No competitor names or price comparisons in public content. No invented numbers.
- Any change of an agreed rule goes into `policies/decisions-log.md` first.
- Source of truth for status: `policies/decisions-log.md`. Plans: `plans/`. Weekly cycle: every Friday 6 PM Cairo time, template in `templates/`.
