# Weekly report and next-week plan (template)

**When:** every Friday at 6 PM (Cairo time). The week runs Saturday to Friday.
**Rule:** every number comes from a record. No record means "no data". Nothing goes out without approval.

## Part 1. What happened this week (Saturday to Friday)
**Every number is shown in three columns: Sara's target, the benchmark (what other companies usually get, if a reliable one exists), and the actual.**

### Marketing
- Posts and videos published vs planned. Link to each.
- Reach and reactions (from the weekly analytics export). Followers gained. Trials with the LinkedIn tracking tag.
- Drafts approved without changes / changed / rejected.
### Sales
- Leads checked, Go / Skip / Founders decide. Skip leads Sara wants to contact.
- Contacted this week (how many, by email or LinkedIn). Replies (yes / no / other). Who did not reply and what we do next. Demos booked.
- Bounces and complaints (stop line status).
### Quality Engineering and Automation
- Module delivered this week and its status (catalog approved, functional tests, end-to-end UI flows, 3 passes in a row).
- Bugs raised, bugs Resolved, bugs retested (verified, reopened), bugs rejected by the developer, duplicates (target zero).
### Usage and time
- Planned vs actual usage per company. Founder time spent on reviews.

## Part 2. Plan for next week (Saturday to Friday)
### Marketing
- Each day: video or post, topic, and the READY drafts and videos attached for approval. Sara decides to publish or change.
### Sales
- Which leads we contact this week, when (day and time), by which channel, and who sends. Follow-up on replies.
### Quality Engineering and Automation
- Which module this week, what happens each day, expected bugs and retests, anything needed from Sara or Abdelrahman.
### Needs from Sara
- Short list of decisions and actions, with a date.

## Part 3. Risks and changes
- What is behind, why, and what we propose (Sara decides).
