## Mon 30 Nov, 1:00 PM (fallback 4:00 PM). POST. Expert. Behind the scenes: how we test every permission
Who can see this ticket? Who can close it? Who can change the settings? In a service desk these questions matter. Before a feature goes out, we test each role and each permission, one case at a time, and we keep the test cases so we can repeat them. This is how we make sure the right person sees the right ticket. Follow along to see how we build.
